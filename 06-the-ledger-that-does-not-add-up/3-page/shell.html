The Payout Desk/Lesson 6

The Ledger That Does Not Add Up

At month-end the treasury ledger shows €50,000 paid out for loan 4471, and the loan ledger shows nothing. Every write moved the right amount. How do you move money between two ledgers so that neither ever shows it twice or not at all?
